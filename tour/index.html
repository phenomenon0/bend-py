How a translation is trusted

1 · elaborate — untrustedPython source becomes a typed IR. Nothing it says is believed; it only proposes.
2 · verify — the kernelEvery claim is recomputed from the IR alone: types, calls, ranks, guards, postconditions. One bad entry refutes all.
3 · emit — or refuseOnly what verified renders as Bend. Everything else is a refusal with a span, the rule, and the repair.

Four lanes per specimen — strict check, interpreter, emitted JS, emitted C — plus CPython as the oracle for answers (C2) and byte-identical emission pins.
